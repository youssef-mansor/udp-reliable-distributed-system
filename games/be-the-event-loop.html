Be the Event Loop

You are a process's single thread: the sender (process 1) in levels 1–3, the receiver (process 2) in level 4. Choose every system call yourself. Wake up on time, never sleep where you can't be woken, and don't burn the CPU asking "anything yet?"

Cheat sheet

poll(.., T) → 1The socket is readable: at least one datagram waits. Returned immediately if one was already there.
poll(.., T) → 0Timeout: T ms passed with nothing. Time for your timers.
poll(.., 0)Check without sleeping. In a loop on an empty buffer, that's busy-waiting.
poll(.., -1)Sleep until a datagram arrives, however long. Your timers can't wake you.
recvfrom → nOne datagram, n bytes. Never more than one per call.
recvfrom → -1 EAGAINNon-blocking socket, buffer empty. Not an error: stop draining.
blocking recvfromOn an empty buffer, sleeps until a datagram arrives. Timers can't wake you.
